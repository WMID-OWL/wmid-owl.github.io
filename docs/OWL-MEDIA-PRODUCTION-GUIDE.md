## 5. Tournament-Specific Presentation Formats

### Bragging Rights Match Edit Posts

For the Bragging Rights tournament only, each match may be presented as a fast-paced, music-driven compilation rather than a full-length match upload.

- Condense each match to approximately 2–3 minutes.
- Prioritize the strongest action, major momentum shifts, signature sequences, and the finish.
- Edit the footage in the style of a social-media wrestling compilation.
- Use the selected music as the primary audio bed instead of producing full match commentary.
- Publish these edits primarily to X/Twitter and Instagram Reels.
- Treat YouTube as a separate version because copyrighted music may trigger Content ID claims, restrictions, or strikes.
- An approximately 30-second original match introduction may be tested before the music-driven edit, but it must not be considered reliable protection against copyright detection.
- This presentation format is exclusive to Bragging Rights and does not replace OWL’s normal match-production format.
