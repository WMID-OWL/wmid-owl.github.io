BEHIND THE BELL

How OWL Simulates

OWL uses a modified Fire Pro Wrestling World setup with official DLC. Every roster member begins at 160 points, with results inside the ring creating permanent progression over time.

160 STARTING POINTS
+20 CHAMPION BOOST
CPU8 125% SPEED

PERMANENT RATING GAINS

  • +1 PPV victory
  • +1 Championship victory
  • +1 Successful championship defense
  • +1 Designated major competition or Signature Series victory
  • +1 Competitively earned official future title opportunity

Categories stack once each per outcome. A standard PPV victory earns +1, while a PPV championship victory or defense earns +2.

Injuries are active, knockouts remain rare, and critical finishes can create storyline absences.
