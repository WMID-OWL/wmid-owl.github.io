ANNUAL TOURNAMENT SYSTEM

PROVING GROUND

No hype. No shortcuts. Just proof. OWL’s annual round-robin tournament rewards performance, points, endurance, and tournament survival.

OWL PG NOV

TOURNAMENT FORMAT

Four divisions. Four entrants each. One proof of greatness.

The Proving Ground is OWL’s World Cup-style tournament. Each show and division produces a four-person block. Wrestlers earn Combat Points through wins, finish methods, speed, and damage before the top two reach the PPV final.

04 Entrants per division
06 Block matches
02 PPV finalists

TOURNAMENT FLOW

From four months of proof to one December title shot.

Proving Ground is not a one-night shortcut. It begins with months of performance, narrows into four-person blocks, and ends with one wrestler earning a December championship opportunity.

QUALIFICATION PATH

Four ways into the block. No free rides.

Every show and division uses the same four-slot entry structure. Two spots reward season-long consistency, one rewards PPV pressure, and one is stolen through the Wildcard Play-In.

BLOCK RESULTS

Every match in the table.

Each four-person block produces six round-robin matches. Results entered here will show the winner, finish method, match time, Combat Points earned, and any major bonuses.

TOURNAMENT FINAL

The last proof before December.

When the block stage ends, the top two point scorers advance to the Proving Grounds PPV final. The winner earns the trophy and a championship opportunity at December to Remember.

COMBAT POINTS

Winning matters. How you win matters more.

Proving Ground matches use a separate tournament scoring system. Standard OWL win-loss records are protected from tournament math, but the tournament table remembers every result.

Win +3
Draw +1
Pinfall +1
Submission +1
KO +2
Under 10 +3
